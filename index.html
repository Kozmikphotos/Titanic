There are two great museums in Ireland dedicated to the Titanic

  • Titanic Belfast
    • Located beside the Titanic Slipways, the Harland & Wolff Drawing Offices and Hamilton Graving Dock – the very place where Titanic was designed, built and launched
  • Titanic Experience Cobh
    • Titanic Experience Cobh is a unique visitor experience located in the historic White Star Line Building – the very place from where Titanic’s last passengers departed
